## Scale, axes and confidence

**All dimensions, coordinates, elevations, exact apertures, light placements and actor transforms below are P: invented prototype proposals, provenance V-01, 2026-10-07, LH_Prototype_v1.** V = clearly visible source feature; I = interpreted connection/function; P = invented for playability. A row marked `V / P metrics` credits the feature, never its dimensions. Source tiles have not been measured.

One layout unit **U = 100 Unreal cm**; construction snaps to 1 U with 2/4 U wall modules and finer aperture offsets as listed. Local +X projects image down-right, +Y image up-right, +Z up; image up is not north. Coordinates are `(X,Y,Z)` in U, with omitted Z equal to finished floor `0`. Rectangle bounds describe clear interior floor. Walls sit outside those bounds; use the shared initial 0.2 U thickness and 4 U full collision height, with visual cutaway/fading independent of collision. Every unlisted boundary is closed.

The W0-04 human ruler is 1.75 U tall with approximate 0.7 U × 1.8 U player collision. Unframed corridor mouths are 3.2 U clear; the stair enclosure aperture is 2.4 U clear × 3 U high. Corridors are 3.2 U clear, stairs 3 U clear, their approach landings at least 3.2 U square. B1's largest proposed static common-enemy footprint is Green Slime at approximately 0.9 U, with Bat wingspan 0.8 U; those are W0-04 proposals, not verified collision. Retain the common kit so later art does not erase clearance. Balork has no B1 placement or required route.

Both stairs are graybox ramps with visual treads, at authored 1:5 and 1:4 slopes respectively; they represent travel portals between separate maps rather than the real vertical separation of floors. Replace the room floor within each ramp footprint; continue side/back blocking masonry down to the low endpoint so the descending ramp is not covered by a Z=0 floor slab or open below a wall. Side masonry lies outside the stated 3 U clear width. Preserve 3 U overhead clearance following each ramp. The terminal back wall closes beyond each trigger, and landing floors remain level. Explicit interaction at the terminal trigger is the candidate travel action, preventing a returning arrival from immediately travelling again; W3-04 owns final behavior and recovery semantics.

Safety is an implementation requirement, not a result inferred from a six-meter rectangle. Mark Entry+C01, Healers+C02, and the descent enclosure as no-spawn/no-patrol/no-pursuit/no-attack volumes; their boundary is the connector's Hub mouth or the enclosure aperture. Reserve the named pads, connecting walk lane and stair approaches free of collision props. W4 must prevent attacks/target acquisition across those safe boundaries, including during load, and avoid enemy parking across the only exit. If those systems are absent, validate with encounter proxies only and keep live encounters disabled; distance from a slot alone does not establish a safe arrival. No healer fees, healing amounts, aggro radii or respawn timers are selected here.

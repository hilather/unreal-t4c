## Builder acceptance and limits

W3-03 should build the room/corridor union first, then the partitions. Walk the complete loop in both directions, visit both dead ends, reach Level 4 without taking the upper-left branch, and return without teleporting across gaps. Sweep the player, Giant Bat and Atrocity proxies through every intended aperture and turn with collision, including C3Cell. The 0.2 U wall allowance must not consume stated clear widths.

Then validate both stair ramps, safe arrivals, two-way paired travel, quit/reload on B3 and destination-failure recovery with W3-04. Test combat retreat along both arms without enemies striking the reserved landing, camera fade at each baffle and controller targeting inside the cell. Compare the blockout at the proposed camera to M3; retain uncertainty on exact historic doors until reviewed. W4 must map all 22 aliases to unique persistent GUIDs and enforce the selected roster without inferring creatures from props.

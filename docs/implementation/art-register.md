## What the images support

The original human directions show base bodies with minimal light garments, exposed limbs, dark hair and a neutral standing stance against black. Direction numbers describe eight views, not eight frames of locomotion. The screen-facing descriptions in the inventory are visual readings, not a recovered world-heading convention. The starter concept adds coarse tunics, trousers, belts, wrist wraps and boots; those costumes and fine facial/hair details are authored interpretations. Use its modest equipment level for the creation presentation, keep appearance independent of attributes, and preserve the design's flexible builds. The warrior/mage/archer promotional names do not authorize permanent classes.

Original creature anchors and concept departures:

## Palette and materials

**Observed:** B1–B3 maps use tightly repeated warm brown/tan masonry and brown floors, with rubble, pale patches and dark red stains. B4 is darker, with visible small amber wall lights. Town interiors show timber/plaster building edges, brown furnishings, gray church paving, a strong red carpet, and green land against blue water. The original low-resolution maps do not establish PBR values, physically measured light levels, wall height or a complete exterior roof design.

**Direction proposal:** use warm brown masonry, rough dark timber, aged off-white plaster, dull leather and coarse linen. Give stone readable large forms and restrained roughness variation. Keep torchlight amber and selective, balanced by dim cooler fill so creatures remain distinct in recesses. Preserve red as a church landmark and red/dark wing patterns for Balork; use green deliberately for slime. Avoid making every creature identifiable only by color.

The environment concept visibly adds slate-like roofs, evening sunlight, a pier/boat, stalls, boundary walls, foliage, beams, barrels, cobwebs and larger torch pools. These are material/dressing proposals, not confirmed church geometry or new required town features. Its central building has a roof opening and its basement has two illustrated stair passages; neither replaces the source nave plan or the actual B1 hub. Limit rubble to edges so it does not erase the reference apertures. Fine fur, translucent slime, luminous eyes, elaborate armour and glowing magic seen in concepts/promotion are optional presentation work, not evidence of original materials or mechanics.
